===== [document] document:consultancy_information.docx | 12. Quality, ethics and responsible AI (739 chars)
Consultancy profile (Word document) > 12. Quality, ethics and responsible AI
Quality and responsible practice are embedded throughout delivery. The exact controls depend on the assignment, but the following principles guide the consultancy’s work.
- Use appropriate data sources and document important assumptions.
- Validate analytical outputs and communicate uncertainty and limitations.
- Protect confidential information and apply sensible access controls.
- Use human review for consequential AI-generated outputs.
- Avoid presenting AI responses as verified facts when evidence is unavailable.
- Design solutions that the client can understand, govern and maintain.
- Provide reproducible methods and documentation where appropriate.
